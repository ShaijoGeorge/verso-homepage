Frequently Asked Questions

What is Verso?

Verso is a simple, dedicated Bible reading companion designed to help you build and maintain a consistent daily reading habit. You mark chapters as you read, keep daily streaks alive, and view your progress with clear, visual charts.

Do I read inside Verso or with my own Bible?

You read from your favorite physical Bible, study Bible, or audio app. Verso serves as your dedicated habit tracker — simply check off the chapters as you complete them.

Is Verso free to use?

Yes, Verso is completely free with no subscription fees, no locked features, and no ads. It was created as a personal passion project to encourage regular Bible reading.

Does Verso work offline?

Yes! You can track your reading offline without an internet connection. Whenever you go back online, your latest progress syncs automatically.

Do I need an account?

Yes. An account is required to use Verso so that your chapters, reading streaks, and progress are securely saved and automatically synced across all your devices.

Is my reading data private?

Yes, 100%. Your reading progress belongs only to you. Verso does not collect personal data to sell, display advertisements, or track you across the web.

Which platforms does Verso support today?

Verso is available today via direct download for Android (APK). Standalone Desktop (Windows) and mobile (iOS) editions are in active development.

Is Verso available on Google Play or the Apple App Store?

Not yet! We are currently releasing Verso directly through our landing page so you can get started right away on Android. Official releases on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store are planned for the future.

How do I install the Android APK directly?

Installing directly takes under 30 seconds:

  1. Tap Download APK in the download section.
  2. When your browser prompts “File might be harmful”, tap Download anyway (this standard Android notice appears for all direct downloads outside Google Play).
  3. Open the downloaded .apk file and tap Install. If prompted, toggle on “Allow from this source”.

Verso is verified, lightweight, and safe.

Can I install Verso on iOS (iPhone or iPad)?

An official iOS version is planned for the Apple App Store in an upcoming release! Because iOS does not support direct website downloads for the general public, iPhone and iPad users will be able to download Verso directly from the App Store once launched.
